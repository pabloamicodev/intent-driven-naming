# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes evidence-based disagreement and contributions from people with different languages, ecosystems, organizations, and levels of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, tradeoffs, and observable outcomes rather than personal qualities.
- Give reviewers enough context to reproduce claims.
- Treat language conventions as contextual engineering decisions, not status markers.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Accept a justified no-op or rejected proposal without hostility.
- Protect private reports, unpublished benchmark data, and contributor information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate benchmark manipulation, impersonation, plagiarism, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove participants and content in proportion to impact and recurrence. Reports should be handled confidentially, with conflicts of interest disclosed and recused where possible.

This policy applies to repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, release channels, and project events.
